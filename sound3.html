    • Tilt to Creak

    Hold your phone flat in your palm, then tilt it left or right like swinging an old door on its hinges. The farther you tilt, the more pronounced the creak becomes. Bring the phone back to level to make it quiet again. The sound lets you hear how far you are tilting without watching the angle readout.

    The program uses the size of the Y-axis tilt to control the position and volume parameters of a Faust door model. Tilts of 12° or less are silent, and the sound parameters reach their maximum at 45°.
